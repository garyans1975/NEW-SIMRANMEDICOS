About Us

22+ Years of Caring for Our Community

What started as a small neighbourhood pharmacy has grown into a name families rely on, generation after generation. Over 22 years, we've built our reputation one prescription, one honest recommendation, and one caring conversation at a time.

We believe a medical store should feel like a place you're looked after, not just served. That's why our team takes the time to guide you, whether it's understanding a prescription or suggesting the right health supplement.

Today, New Simran Medicos continues that same promise — genuine products, fair prices, and a team that genuinely cares.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes, we offer quick home delivery in the local area. Simply message us your requirement on WhatsApp with your address.

Do I need a prescription for all medicines?

Scheduled medicines require a valid doctor's prescription as per regulations. Over-the-counter items and general health products don't need one.

What are your store timings?

We're open 7 days a week from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Do you keep Ayurvedic medicines and other essentials too?

Yes! Alongside Allopathic medicines, we stock Ayurvedic products, surgical items, baby foods and cosmetics from trusted brands.

How can I place an order?

The easiest way is to tap the WhatsApp button and send us a photo of your prescription or a list of what you need.
